Who it's for

For anyone who wants to learn ragtime — the cheerful, bouncy music that gave birth to jazz — from scratch and at any age. If you have a keyboard and a computer, you have everything. The exercises start from the first touch and build up to a real ragtime strain.

What's inside

10

Progressive exercises

From the basic stride to syncopation, the cakewalk, up to a full strain with a turnaround.

2

Hands separated

Left (stride) and right (syncopated melody) on distinct rows, so you can study one at a time.

✓

Checked

Every right-hand note belongs to the bar's chord: automatic harmonic check.

How it works

  • Download the course and one of the apps below.
  • Open an exercise: watch the notes fall, one row for the right hand and one for the left.
  • With wait mode, the notes wait for you: you never fall behind.
  • Study the left-hand stride first, alone and slow. Then add the right hand.

Which app to play with
